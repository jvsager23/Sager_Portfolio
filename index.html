Campaign Case Study

03 / CAMPAIGN LOG
Fil-Estate CORE, Inc. · Leon Philippe Farms — Meta Ads & Organic Growth
Brief

A veteran broker network needed a modern way to recruit — one that didn't rely on cold calls or referrals alone. Separately, Leon Philippe Farms needed a sales channel that didn't depend on ad spend.

Approach

I ran broker-recruitment campaigns on Meta Ads with a deliberately lean ₱2,000/month budget, while producing the property photography, drone footage, and video assets to fuel the creative. Alongside the paid work, I ran marketing and sales seminars to bring veteran brokers up to speed on social selling, and managed organic content across Fil-Estate Core, Leon Philippe Farms, and The Weekends El Nido.

Result

The campaign generated 200+ broker recruitment leads over 6 months at roughly ₱14 per messaging result — efficient enough to sustain on a small budget. On the organic side, Leon Philippe Farms reached about ₱5,000/month in product sales with zero paid spend, driven entirely by content and community management.
